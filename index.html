Travel With Respect for Sabah

Protect habitats, respect communities, choose responsible services and follow current local guidance wherever your journey takes you.

  • ✓ Respect Wildlife and Protected Places — Maintain distance, follow guidelines and never feed or touch wildlife.
  • ✓ Support Local Communities — Choose locally-owned services and purchase authentic crafts directly from makers.
  • ✓ Use Verified and Responsible Services — Look for STEP accreditation and choose operators committed to sustainable practices.
