# GoSabah.com — Brand Identity Manual (Proposed CI System, September 2026)

Distilled from `gosabahbrandguideline.pdf` (12 pages, "Internal working standard — Proposed brand
story and CI framework"). Tagline: **"Stories that move with Sabah."** — *A visual language for
discovery, belonging and the next journey.*

> **Status:** this is a *proposed* CI system, not production-locked. The document itself flags what
> is still open — see §7 "Not yet locked" below. Treat every value here as the working standard
> until vector masters and physical/production proofs confirm it.

## 1. Brand story — "The Journey Bird"

The mark is a bird lifting from the landscape, carrying a sense of Sabah with it.

> **"Go" is the invitation. "Sabah" is the feeling.**

Three ideas held in one symbol:
- **Lift** — the sweeping lower stroke gives momentum: a journey beginning, a wing rising, a route opening forward.
- **Belong** — the enclosing circle creates a sense of place and continuity: a world that can be entered, explored, remembered.
- **Tell** — the white negative space keeps the mark open and human, leaving room for interpretation, like every traveller completing the story their own way.

## 2. Brand platform

**Identity in one sentence:** *GoSabah.com makes Sabah easier to feel, explore and share.*

- **Brand role:** the welcoming guide between curiosity and experience — moves people from "I have heard of Sabah" to "I know where I want to go, what I want to feel, and why it matters."
- **Brand promise:** every interaction should make Sabah feel closer, more vivid, more possible — without flattening its complexity or turning it into a cliché.
- **Brand character:** Warm · Curious · Grounded · Optimistic · Useful · Proud
- **What we are NOT:** a loud bargain site. A generic tropical postcard. A distant institutional voice. A promise of perfection that ignores real people, weather, distance and responsibility.
- **Design test:** when a piece of communication is in doubt, ask — *does this help someone go, feel, understand or remember Sabah?* If it does none of those things, simplify it.

## 3. Logo system

- **Journey symbol:** a circular orange-to-coral gesture enclosing a white bird-like form — a place seen from above, a wing in motion, a route still unfolding.
- **Wordmark:** "GO" sits above "Sabah" as a clear call to action; deep green + brown anchor the energy of the symbol in nature, land and local warmth. ".com" appears in orange.
- **⚠️ The wordmark must be treated as artwork — never recreate it by typing the name in a similar font.** The exact typeface of the supplied wordmark is not confirmed from the raster source.

### Lockups & hierarchy
| Lockup | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Primary / full lockup** (symbol + "GO Sabah.com") | Website mastheads, campaigns, brochures, partner communications, first-touch brand moments — the default |
| **Secondary / symbol only** | Favicon, social avatar, app tile, watermark, badge, compact navigation — replace with approved vector symbol when available |

Reading order: 1) **First touch** — full lockup, "GO" visibly above "Sabah". 2) **Repeated touch** — symbol + short-form "GoSabah" once recognition is established. 3) **Small touch** — symbol only, provided surrounding context identifies GoSabah.com.

### Clear space
Minimum clear space = **1× the height of the "G" in "GO"** on all four sides. When in doubt, add more space.
- **Partner lockups:** separate from partner logos with a vertical rule or generous neutral gap — never let marks touch.
- **Photography:** keep the clear-space area free of faces, high-contrast edges, horizon lines and important destination details.
- **Digital UI:** minimum 16px internal breathing room in compact headers, scaling proportionally on larger screens.

### Minimum size
| Digital | Min height | | Print | Min height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desktop header | 32px | | General print | 12mm full lockup |
| Mobile header | 26px | | Small stationery | 10mm full lockup |
| Social avatar / symbol | 48px | | Embroidery / etching | Symbol-only, test first |
| Favicon / app tile | 16px symbol-only | | Large format | Optical scale to context |

**Responsive rule:** below minimum size, remove complexity before reducing legibility — switch to the symbol-only variant rather than shrinking the full lockup until ".com" becomes noise.

**Production check before release:** test the mark at actual size on a phone screen, matte paper, coated paper, fabric, one-colour vinyl and a dark photograph. If the bird silhouette or ".com" disappears, use the responsive variant.

**Never improvise colourways** — maintain a controlled set of full-colour, flat-colour, one-colour and reverse files.

## 4. Colour system

*A palette with a sense of place: orange brings movement; olive and earth hold the story; white keeps the experience open.*

### Primary palette
| Name | Hex | Role | Recommended share | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rainforest Olive** | `#3F562A` | Primary anchor, navigation, headings | 20–30% | Living landscape, trust, grounded expertise |
| **Borneo Earth** | `#42281A` | Wordmark tone, long-form text, heritage moments | 10–20% | Soil, craft, local warmth and permanence |
| **Journey Orange** | `#F28C48` | Calls to action, highlights, route markers | 5–10% | Invitation, energy, first step |
| **Sunset Coral** | `#F35B63` | Gradient partner, editorial emphasis | 2–6% | Human emotion, sunset, memory |

### Supporting palette
| Name | Hex | Role | Recommended share | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sea Route Teal** | `#176B6C` | Secondary action, water and conservation content | 3–8% | Coast, clarity, calm confidence |
| **Sand Mist** | `#F4EDE3` | Backgrounds and breathing room | 35–55% | Light, air, restraint, editorial calm |
| **Cloud White** | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds and breathing room | (see Sand Mist) | Light, air, restraint, editorial calm |
| **Leaf Tint** | `#E9EFE4` | Subtle fills / callout backgrounds | — | Soft landscape accent |

### Rules
- **Gradient rule:** use the orange-to-coral gradient for the symbol, hero lines, feature moments and celebratory campaigns — it should feel like light moving across a journey, not a default background on every surface.
- **Contrast rule:** white type on olive, earth or teal. Earth or olive type on white/sand. Avoid coral text for long paragraphs — reserve coral for accents only.
- **Colourways:** Full colour on white (default/preferred) · Reverse on olive · Reverse on earth (best for premium/heritage/night applications) · Full colour on Sand Mist (warmer editorial field — preserve contrast around ".com").
- ⚠️ These hex values are working digital swatches *sampled and interpreted from the supplied raster logo* — confirm final RGB/CMYK/spot values against the approved vector master and physical proofs before production.

## 6. Photography

The brand CI PDF itself contains only one placement rule: **keep the logo's clear-space area free of
faces, high-contrast edges, horizon lines and important destination details.** Style direction below
was given directly during this build (Sept 2026) and supersedes that gap.

### Style direction — what to use

- **Golden/blue hour light**, not flat midday sun — light with direction and warmth.
- **Confident, considered colour grading** — rich, lively colour that still reads as a real place,
  not an unedited flat/dull straight-out-of-camera shot.
- **Deliberate angle and composition** — a chosen viewpoint, not a snapshot with no framing intent.
- **Immediate impact** — the image should create anticipation for Sabah on first glance, not sit flat.
- **Living subjects (wildlife, people) must be tack-sharp and clearly the focal point** — the
  surrounding environment can carry mood/atmosphere, but the animal or person itself should be in
  crisp focus, not lost in soft-focus/bokeh ambiguity.

### Style direction — what to avoid

- Flat, dull, harsh midday light with no golden-hour warmth.
- Unedited, uncorrected, flat-colour images with no grading.
- No compositional intent — snapshots taken from an arbitrary angle.
- (Carried over from the IA submission paper, §21) Overprocessed HDR or fake teal-orange grading —
  "lively and well-graded" is not the same as "cranked and artificial."

### Hard requirements — non-negotiable regardless of style

These come from the project's own media-governance rules (`docs/gosabah-project-brief.md` §9) and
were reconfirmed by two batches of example images sent during this build that violated them:

- **Every image must be verifiably Sabah.** Two of the reference examples given were not Sabah at
  all — a temple that is Doi Inthanon, Thailand, and a limestone-cliff lagoon that is Coron/Palawan,
  Philippines. Recognisable non-Sabah landmarks or landscapes must never stand in for Sabah, no
  matter how visually appealing.
- **No AI-generated imagery representing real people, culture or places.** One example was
  explicitly labelled "AI-generated" — a fabricated depiction of a traditional dance/community. This
  is a hard no: inventing a scene of a living, real, consent-requiring culture is inaccurate,
  undignified, and exactly what the governance rules exist to prevent for an official government
  tourism platform.
- **No watermarked or unlicensed stock.** Several examples carried a visible "Magnific" (AI
  upscaling tool) watermark — unverified provenance, unusable regardless of how it looks.
- Every asset still needs the full rights record from §9: creator, rights owner, licence,
  attribution, permitted uses, expiry/withdrawal condition.
- **Species accuracy matters as much as location.** A third batch of examples included a Malayan
  tapir and a spider monkey as "good style" references — both fine as *style-only* references (sharp
  focus, natural light) but neither is a Sabah/Bornean species: tapirs in Malaysia range Peninsular
  Malaysia/Sumatra, not Borneo; spider monkeys exist only in Central/South America. Real Sabah
  wildlife imagery must show actual Bornean species — orangutan, proboscis monkey, Bornean pygmy
  elephant, sun bear, hornbill, etc. — never a visually-similar substitute from elsewhere. The same
  batch also included another likely non-Sabah overwater-bungalow cove (Coron/Palawan-style
  architecture) and another "Magnific"-watermarked sunset — same two problems as before, still
  disqualifying regardless of how well they match the style direction.

### Sourcing

Sabah Tourism Board's own published photo library was flagged as a source
(`media.borneophotos.com`) but this session's sandbox network policy blocks that domain outright —
not a retryable failure. To use it: either the user fetches/downloads specific images and uploads
them as files (as with the PDFs), or a session with that domain allowlisted pulls them in.
